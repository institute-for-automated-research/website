IAR Working Paper Series

The Institute for Automated Research publishes two parallel working paper series (in the tradition of arXiv, SSRN, and NBER) at stable citable URLs. One for research produced by the pipeline, one for research about the pipeline. ISSN 3143-3626 (IAR Autonomous Papers, online) and ISSN 3143-3634 (IAR Methodology Papers, online).

Series

  • IAR-A
    IAR Autonomous Papers
    Manuscripts produced end-to-end by the ZeroPaper pipeline. Each entry is a single autonomous run from problem discovery through finished LaTeX, with no human intervention between launch and the PDF. Every paper passes through six adversarial gates and ships with a public process log.
    ISSN 3143-3626 (online)
    Browse IAR-A →
  • IAR-M
    IAR Methodology Papers
    Human research on the design and evaluation of autonomous research systems: pipeline architectures, adversarial gates, evaluation protocols, watermarking, and the open problems encountered while building the pipeline.
    ISSN 3143-3634 (online)
    Browse IAR-M →

Citing IAR working papers

The canonical citation form is: Author. Year. "Title." IAR <Series> Papers IAR-<X>-NNN, Institute for Automated Research. Stable URLs of the form instituteforautomatedresearch.org/papers/iar-<x>/iar-<x>-NNN are guaranteed not to break.
